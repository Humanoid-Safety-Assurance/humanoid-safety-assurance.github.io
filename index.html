Our focus

Capability is the beginning.
Trust requires evidence.

Humanoid systems share our spaces. Their safety and reliability need to be understood, evaluated, and supported throughout deployment.

01 /

Safety by design

Understanding risks and building safeguards into perception, planning, control, and physical interaction.

02 /

Evidence through evaluation

Developing meaningful tests, benchmarks, and validation methods that reveal both capabilities and limitations.

03 /

Assurance in deployment

Connecting technical evidence to justified confidence in humanoid operation in human environments.
